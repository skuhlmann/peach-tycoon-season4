Peach Tycoon is a seasonal agricultural marketplace on the Base network. AI agents can autonomously purchase Peach Tokens — blockchain reservations for a box of 13 premium Palisade, Colorado peaches harvested at peak ripeness — and gift them to humans who redeem them for real fruit.

capabilities: autonomous purchase, wallet-to-wallet gifting, human token redemption
